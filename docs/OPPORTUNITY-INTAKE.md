(~90 min), the CT college fair, and the Arkansas course if it turns out to be
Arkansas-only. Not useless, but they should not rank above local ones.

hospital volunteering. Worth confirming, because listing them for a 14-year-old
is the exact failure we built the age engine to prevent.

**Two are school-mediated, not individual.** Meadowlands and Waterloo CEMC. The
card has to say "ask a teacher" rather than "apply", or a teen wastes their time.

**Three have fees I could not confirm.** CMUNCE delegate fee, CEMC per-student
fee, the film festival submission fee. All get `cost_unconfirmed` until read.

Library (Hudson County, grade 7, free, gives a letter), Hackensack Riverkeeper
